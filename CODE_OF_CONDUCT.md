# Code of Conduct — maatify/logger

[![Maatify Logger](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-Logger-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify/Logger)
[![Maatify Ecosystem](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-Ecosystem-9C27B0?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify)

## Participation

Take part respectfully and professionally. Make room for good-faith technical disagreement, discuss ideas and evidence, and address people with courtesy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxing, intimidation, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and official project channels.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@maatify.dev](mailto:support@maatify.dev). Maintainers may moderate or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
